Platform Overview

Bridging the Divide Between Rural Harvests and Commercial Trade

Kisan Connect was built to eliminate traditional trade fragmentation and price opacity. By providing agricultural officers and farmers with direct registration tools, the platform digitizes farm acreages, seasonal yields, and farmer-expected crop prices.

Commercial traders and institutional buyers gain authenticated access to verified crop listings, enabling direct purchase enquiries backed by real-time APMC wholesale market price intelligence.

Core Architecture

Four Pillars of the Platform

An integrated framework designed to organize agricultural data, empower rural producers, and facilitate transparent market procurement.

01
👨‍🌾

Farmer Registration

Maintain verified farmer profiles, contact details, total landholding acreage, and state-and-district geographic mapping to establish an authentic agricultural registry.

02
🌱

Crop Management

Register seasonal crops across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons, detailing planted acreage, expected quintal yields, harvest schedules, and farmer-specified expected purchase prices.

03
📊

Market Price Discovery

Access daily benchmark wholesale market crop rates across agricultural trading hubs, giving stakeholders real-time minimum, maximum, and modal price visibility.

04
🤝

Purchase Enquiries

Connect verified buyers and farmers through formal purchase enquiries specifying required quantities, offered unit prices, and custom terms, monitored directly by operators.
